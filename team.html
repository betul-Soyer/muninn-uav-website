    The people

    The Team

    Seven students, three subteams, one aircraft. Muninn UAV is the aerospace team of the Yazgit Community at Konya Technical University. This page is who we are, how we are organized and how to reach us.

    Our story

    The Yazgit Community's aerospace team

    Muninn UAV was born inside the Yazgit Community at Konya Technical University. The name comes from the raven that flies out each day and brings memory home — fitting for a team whose first rule is that nothing counts until it is written down. What unites us is one obsession: aircraft that fly their own mission, and the perception problem at the heart of SUAS — reliably finding small objects on the ground from the air.

    We are organized like the aircraft we build: small, modular and honest about interfaces. Three subteams own their subsystems end-to-end, and every subsystem must prove itself in our staged test program before it flies. Decisions are written down — the important ones are public in our design decision log.

    How we work

    Three subteams, clear interfaces

    Software

    The autonomy stack: PX4 configuration, ROS 2, the custom-dataset YOLOv8s detector, SLAM mapping, visual-servoing alignment, the Gazebo digital twin and mission setup in QGroundControl.

    Electrical

    Avionics integration, wiring harness, power system, the three radio links — everything on board that carries current or a signal.

    Mechanical

    The tail-sitter airframe: structure, wingtip landing fins, the center pod and manufacturing.
