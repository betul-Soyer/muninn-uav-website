    Our story

    The Yazgit Community's aerospace team

    Muninn UAV is the aerospace team of the Yazgit Community at Konya Technical University, named after the raven of Norse myth that flies out each day and returns with what it has seen. The team works on autonomous flight and on the perception problem at the centre of the SUAS mission: finding small objects on the ground from the air.

    Three subteams — avionics, software/AI and mechanical — own their subsystems end to end. Every subsystem has to pass the staged test programme before it flies, and the reasoning behind each major choice is published in the design decision log.
