    GöğeBakan flies on engineering discipline and on the generosity of people who fund student hardware. Here is who backs us — and how to join them for the SUAS 2026 season.

    SUAS 2026 season

    Our sponsors

    TÜBİTAK already supports the project, and founding sponsor slots are open. Logos placed now stay for the entire campaign — through SUAS 2026 this September.

    The case

    Why sponsor a student UAS team

    Visibility that flies

    Your logo on an autonomous HSVTOL aircraft, on team apparel, on this website and in every video we publish — culminating at an international competition in the USA.

    Access to talent

    Seven students who design, build, test and document real aerospace hardware under deadline. Sponsors meet the team first — before recruiting season does.

    Accountable support

    Monthly engineering reports, a transparent budget and a public build log. You will always know what your support built.

    In-kind

    Support that isn't money

    Some of our most valuable sponsorships never touch a bank account.

    Materials

    Carbon tubes and plates, composites, foam stock, 3D-printing filament and resin.

    Manufacturing

    CNC machining time, laser cutting, industrial 3D printing for the center pod and structural parts.

    Electronics

    Batteries, motors, ESCs, radio gear — or an engineer's afternoon reviewing our harness.

    Flight fields

    Safe, legal test field access near our base turns directly into more flight hours.

    Travel & logistics

    Flights, freight and lodging for the trip to SUAS 2026 in September.

    Expertise

    Design reviews from practicing engineers — we trade progress reports for hard questions.
