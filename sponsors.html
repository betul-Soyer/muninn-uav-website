    SUAS 2026 season

    Our sponsors

    TÜBİTAK, Girişimci İşadamları Vakfı Konya and Kapsül Teknoloji Platformu already support the project, and founding sponsor slots are open. Logos placed now stay for the entire campaign — through SUAS 2026 this September.

    Materials & time

    In-kind support

    Materials, machine time and expertise are as useful to us as funding.

    Materials

    Carbon tubes and plates, composites, foam stock, 3D-printing filament and resin.

    Manufacturing

    CNC machining time, laser cutting, industrial 3D printing for the center pod and structural parts.

    Electronics

    Batteries, motors, ESCs, radio gear — or an engineer's afternoon reviewing our harness.

    Flight fields

    Safe, legal test field access near our base turns directly into more flight hours.

    Travel & logistics

    Flights, freight and lodging for the trip to SUAS 2026 in September.

    Expertise

    Design reviews from practicing engineers — we trade progress reports for hard questions.

    Get in touch

    Write to us and we will send the sponsorship deck and the season budget, and arrange a call with the team captain.
